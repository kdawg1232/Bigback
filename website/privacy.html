PRIVACY

The No-Snitching Manifesto

Big Back was built on one principle: Honesty shouldn't be shared.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. We do not have servers. We do not have accounts. Every piece of data you enter—every burger, every dollar, every timestamp—is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

2. Data Usage

Your data is used exclusively to generate your personal statistics and receipts within the app. We cannot see it. We cannot sell it. We cannot track it.

3. Third Parties

We do not share data with any third parties because we don't have your data to begin with.

4. Data Deletion

You have full control. You can wipe all your data at any time by using the "Clear My Sins" button in the app settings. This action is permanent and cannot be undone by us, as we have no backups.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach out via the Support page.
