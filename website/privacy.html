PRIVACY

The No-Snitching Manifesto

Big Back was built on one principle: Honesty shouldn't be shared.

Big Back does not run its own servers for your spend log. We do not require an account. The amounts, brands, and stats you save are stored on your device (for example using on-device storage). We do not receive that information on our systems because we do not operate a backend that collects it.

1. Optional location and geofencing (iOS)

If you turn on restaurant detection in the app, Big Back may use precise location on your device to:

  • Find places (for example fast-food brands) near you so the app can set up geofences around those spots.
  • Detect when you enter or leave those areas so we can offer a local reminder to log what you spent (after a short delay).

Background / “Always” access: Geofencing can fire when the app is not open. That is why the app may ask for location while using the app and, if you approve it, always-on location on iOS. You can refuse or later turn this off in Settings inside the app and in system Settings.

Apple’s services: To discover nearby venues, the app uses Apple MapKit location search on-device. Those searches are handled by Apple’s systems under Apple’s privacy terms—not by Big Back’s servers. Location and spend data you keep in the app are still not uploaded to us because we do not operate a service that receives them.

If you do not enable restaurant detection, we do not use location for that feature.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may schedule reminders on your device (for example weekly check-ins and optional geofence-related prompts). We do not use those permissions to run ads for third parties or to sell notification access.

3. Camera and photos

If you use receipt scanning or choose images from your library, the app accesses your camera and/or photo library only to process what you choose in the app. That processing happens on your device. We do not operate a cloud archive of your photos or receipts.

4. Data we do not collect (as an app publisher)

We do not ask you to sign in to Big Back. We do not maintain user accounts in the app for your spending log. We do not sell your spend log or location history because we do not operate the infrastructure that would receive those from the app.

5. Data usage on your phone

Your logged spending and preferences are used only to power the app’s screens and statistics on your device. Apple and your device OS may provide services (location, maps search, notifications) according to their own policies.

6. Third parties

Apart from platform services described above (for example Apple), we do not share your spending data with advertisers or data brokers—we do not have that data on our servers to share.

7. Children

Big Back is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own backend services.

8. Data deletion

You can remove your data from the device at any time using options in the app (for example clearing stored data in settings where available). Because we do not host your log on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete remotely for that local content.

9. App Store and “App Privacy” labels

When you submit or update the app, Apple’s App Store Connect may ask what data types are linked to the user. You should declare data types consistent with how the app actually behaves—for example precise location if restaurant detection is used, with a purpose such as app functionality or reminders—and match this policy and your in-app explanations.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach out via the Support page.
